

Customer experience
- Open the bot and tap Book Now.
- Select a service and available time.
- Enter or share their name and phone number.
- Select a payment method and upload a screenshot.
- Receive a booking reference and status updates.

Barber/admin experience
- View today's appointments and upcoming bookings.
- Approve or reject payment proofs.
- Mark appointments as confirmed, completed, or cancelled.
- Change services, prices, opening hours, and payment accounts.
- Publish discounts, announcements, and support information.
